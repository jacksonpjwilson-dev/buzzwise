NAME: Family Game Night
DIFFICULTY: easy
TAGLINE: Gentle questions for all ages
== R1
# Ocean Life
What is the largest fish in the sea? | Whale shark
Which ocean animal squirts ink and has eight arms? | Octopus
What sea creature has five arms and can regrow them? | Starfish (sea star)
What marine mammal is known for its intelligence and clicking sounds? | Dolphin
What do you call a group of fish swimming together? | School
# Dinosaurs
Which dinosaur's name means "tyrant lizard king"? | Tyrannosaurus rex
Which dinosaur had three horns on its face? | Triceratops
Which plant-eater had plates along its back and spikes on its tail? | Stegosaurus
What do scientists who study fossils call themselves? | Paleontologists
What 1993 movie featured dinosaurs brought back to life on an island? | Jurassic Park
# Food Around the World
What Italian dish is a flat round dough topped with sauce and cheese? | Pizza
Sushi comes from which country? | Japan
What Mexican dish is a folded tortilla with fillings? | Taco
What French bread is long and thin? | Baguette
What Belgian breakfast treat has a grid pattern? | Waffle
# Video Games
What plumber is Nintendo's mascot? | Mario
What is the name of Sonic's two-tailed fox sidekick? | Tails
In Minecraft, what green creature explodes? | Creeper
What falling-block puzzle game came from the Soviet Union? | Tetris
What yellow character eats dots and avoids ghosts? | Pac-Man
# Transportation
What vehicle travels on rails? | Train
What do you call a boat that travels underwater? | Submarine
What two-wheeled vehicle do you pedal? | Bicycle
What aircraft uses spinning rotors on top to fly? | Helicopter
What vehicle has a siren and rushes people to the hospital? | Ambulance
# Silly Rhymes
Rhymes with "goat": a vehicle that floats | Boat
Rhymes with "rain": something that runs on tracks and has a caboose | Choo-choo train
Rhymes with "moon": a utensil for soup | Spoon
Rhymes with "cake": a body of water smaller than a sea | Lake
Rhymes with "bear": a fruit shaped like a light bulb | Pear
== R2
# Pets
What pet is known as "man's best friend"? | Dog
What small rodent pet stores food in its cheeks? | Hamster
What pet purrs when happy? | Cat
What is a baby dog called? | Puppy
What pet lives in a bowl and is often orange? | Goldfish
# Science Basics
What gas do plants take in from the air? | Carbon dioxide
At what temperature in Celsius does water freeze? | Zero degrees
What force pulls objects toward Earth? | Gravity
What do you call water in its gas form? | Steam (water vapor)
What is the center of an atom called? | Nucleus
# Kids' Books
Which bear loves honey and lives in the Hundred Acre Wood? | Winnie the Pooh
In "Charlotte's Web," what is the name of the pig? | Wilbur
What hungry insect eats through many foods in Eric Carle's book? | The Very Hungry Caterpillar
What is the name of the boy who never grows up? | Peter Pan
Which girl falls down a rabbit hole into Wonderland? | Alice
# Seasons
In which season do leaves change color and fall? | Autumn
In which season is the longest day of the year (Northern Hemisphere)? | Summer
What do bears do through the winter? | Hibernate
What month does spring officially begin in the Northern Hemisphere? | March
What do you call frozen water falling as flakes? | Snow
# Magic & Wizards
What does a magician traditionally pull out of a hat? | Rabbit
What word do magicians say along with "hocus pocus"? | Abracadabra
What is the name of the wizard in The Lord of the Rings who says "You shall not pass"? | Gandalf
What object does a witch fly on? | Broomstick
Who is the wizard at the end of the Yellow Brick Road? | The Wizard of Oz
# Around Town
Where do you go to borrow books for free? | Library
Where would you see a doctor in an emergency? | Hospital
Where do firefighters work? | Fire station
Where do you go to see animals from around the world? | Zoo
Where would you go to buy stamps and mail packages? | Post office
