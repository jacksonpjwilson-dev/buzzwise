NAME: Thanksgiving Feast
DIFFICULTY: medium
TAGLINE: Gobble up some holiday trivia
== R1
# Pilgrims & History
What ship carried the Pilgrims to America in 1620? | Mayflower
Where did the Pilgrims establish their colony? | Plymouth
Which Native American tribe shared the 1621 harvest feast? | Wampanoag
Which Patuxet man helped the Pilgrims and spoke English? | Squanto (Tisquantum)
Which magazine editor campaigned for years to make Thanksgiving a national holiday? | Sarah Josepha Hale
# Side Dishes
What tart red berry sauce is a Thanksgiving staple? | Cranberry sauce
What bread-based dish is often cooked inside the turkey? | Stuffing
Which orange vegetable is often topped with marshmallows? | Sweet potatoes
Which canned soup is the base of classic green bean casserole? | Cream of mushroom soup
What starchy side is often whipped with butter and milk? | Mashed potatoes
# Pies
What orange squash is the most famous Thanksgiving pie? | Pumpkin
What nut pie is a Southern favorite? | Pecan
What custard-filled pie is topped with toasted meringue and citrus? | Lemon meringue
What is the crumbly topping on many fruit pies called? | Streusel
What whipped topping goes on pumpkin pie? | Whipped cream
# Parades & Football
Which store sponsors the famous NYC Thanksgiving parade? | Macy's
Which NFL team plays every Thanksgiving at home in Michigan? | Detroit Lions
Which NFL team traditionally hosts a Thanksgiving game in Texas? | Dallas Cowboys
What are the giant figures floating in the parade? | Balloons
Who arrives at the end of the Macy's parade? | Santa Claus
# Fall Harvest
What is a "cornucopia" also called? | Horn of plenty
What gourd-like orange fruits are picked from a patch in October? | Pumpkins (patch)
What tree's leaves are on Canada's flag? | Maple
What do you call the colorful dried corn used as decoration? | Flint corn (Indian corn)
What drink is made from pressed apples in the fall? | Apple cider
# Turkey Facts
What is a female turkey called? | Hen
What is the fleshy thing hanging over a turkey's beak? | Snood
What sound does a turkey make? | Gobble
What is a baby turkey called? | Poult
What is the bone pulled for good luck? | Wishbone
